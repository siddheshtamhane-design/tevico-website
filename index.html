Questions, answered.

What is Tevico?

Tevico is a CloudOps platform by Comprinno that brings cost, security, reliability and compliance under one owner. The platform runs continuous checks and fixes, backed by a proven process and certified experts.

What's the difference between standard and advanced assessments?

Standard assessments cover the core best-practice checks across cost, security and reliability. Advanced assessments go deeper into workload-specific configuration, compliance frameworks and architecture review.

Is Tevico a value add beyond my cloud provider's dashboards?

Provider dashboards show you data. Tevico acts on it: it prioritises findings, applies fixes, reports on outcomes and keeps re-checking, with experts on hand for the calls that need judgement.

Is it safe for regulated industries?

Tevico connects with read-only access by default, and every change that matters waits for your approval. Audit-ready reports are produced for the compliance frameworks you follow.

What will logging cost?

Logging costs depend on your volume and retention. Tevico surfaces those costs alongside your other spend and recommends retention and routing settings to keep them in check.

Can I run CI/CD or infrastructure orchestration?

Yes. Our DevOps practice covers CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure as code. Talk to us about your setup and we'll scope it with you.
